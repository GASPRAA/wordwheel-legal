Request Account Deletion

If you wish to permanently delete your Word Wheel Live account and all associated data, you can do so in two ways:

Option 1: Inside the App (Fastest)

  1. Open the Word Wheel Live app.
  2. Go to Settings from the bottom menu.
  3. Tap on Delete Account under Account Management.
  4. Confirm your decision. Your data will be deleted instantly.

Option 2: Via Email Request

If you cannot access the app, you can request account deletion by sending us an email from the address associated with your account.

Request Deletion via Email
Warning: Account deletion is irreversible. All your Gold, Ranking Points, VIP status, and match history will be permanently erased from our servers.
